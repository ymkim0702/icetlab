01 / Research

From understanding to engineering.

An immune response depends not only on what cells do, but also on where and when they act. Our research connects fundamental immunology with functional genomics and synthetic biology to study and reshape immune cell behaviors.

01 / DECODE

Immune responses in
health & disease

Investigating immune cell behavior and function in homeostasis and inflammation, with a focus on cancer, infection, lung inflammation, aging, and tissue de/re-generation.

Tissue immunity
Fundamental immunology
02 / ENGINEER

Natural & Engineered immunity

Using functional genomics—including CRISPR-based screening and gene editing—and synthetic biology to identify genes and proteins that shape how immune cells sense, navigate, and adapt to the tissue environment.

Functional genomics &
Synthetic biology
03 / TRANSLATION

Novel gene & cell therapies

Developing a novel framework for spatially targeted gene & cell therapies for cancer and inflammatory diseases, integrating CRISPR, cancer vaccine, CAR, and protein engineering approaches.

Translational immunotherapy
